Mailkey app package
===================

Files: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and three icons. Keep them together in one folder.

1) Put the folder online (free, any one of these):
   - Netlify Drop: open app.netlify.com/drop and drop the unzipped folder.
   - Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages: upload the files to a new project.
   The site must use https.

2) Install on your phone:
   - Open the site in Chrome, tap the three dots, then "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
   - Mailkey now opens in its own window with its own icon and works offline after the first visit.

3) Want a real .apk file? After step 1, enter your site address at pwabuilder.com and choose Android.

Nothing in this app saves or sends your master secret, Gmail addresses or passwords.
The offline copy only stores the app files.
