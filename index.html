Dots and +tags are ignored for Gmail, as Google does.

Good to know

  • If someone learns one of your passwords, a short secret can be guessed from it. Make the secret long.
  • Write down the version you use for each Gmail. A different version gives a different password.
  • Everything is calculated on this device. This page saves and sends nothing.
  • Also turn on 2-step verification for each Gmail account.
